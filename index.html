PGDEMS COURSE

Post Graduate Diploma in Emergency Medical Services

🎓 Eligibility

  • MBBS
  • BDS
  • BPTH
  • BAMS
  • BHMS
  • BUMS
  • B.Sc. Nursing

🎯 Course Objectives

  • Medical आणि Trauma Emergencies हाताळण्यातील proficiency.
  • Current protocols ची माहिती.
  • Platinum Ten Minutes आणि Golden Hour concepts.
  • Mass disaster मध्ये first responder म्हणून काम करण्याची तयारी.
  • Pre-hospital setting मध्ये medical relief देण्याची क्षमता.

📝 Examination

Modular tests आणि Final Examination.

Final Examination: 200 Marks

Theory: 100 | Practical: 100

Passing Score: किमान 50%

COURSE CURRICULUM

अभ्यासक्रमातील प्रमुख विषय

Module 1 – Preparatory

EMS System: Roles & Responsibilities, Pathophysiology, Pharmacology.

Module 2 – Patient Assessment

History Taking, Physical Examination Techniques आणि Patient Assessment.

Module 3 – Basic Life Support

Basic Life Support (BLS).

Module 4 – Airway Management

Airway Management and Ventilation.

Module 5 – Trauma

Trauma Systems, Mechanism of Injury, Hemorrhage & Shock, Soft Tissue, Head & Facial, Spinal, Thoracic, Abdominal आणि Musculoskeletal Trauma.

Module 6 – Medical Emergency

Pulmonary, Cardiology, Neurology, Gastroenterology, Endocrinology, Urology/Renal, Allergies & Anaphylaxis, Hematology, Toxicology, Environmental Conditions, Gynecology & Obstetrics, Infectious Diseases आणि Behavioral/Psychiatric Disorders.

Module 7 – Advanced Cardiac Life Support

Tachycardia, Bradycardia, Cardiac Arrest आणि Post Cardiac Arrest Care.

📋 Logbook & Assessment

Clinical rotations आणि field training साठी logbook maintain करण्याची तरतूद.

👨‍⚕️ Practical Learning

Lectures, manikins वर practical training आणि clinical rotations यांच्या माध्यमातून skills development.
